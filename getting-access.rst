Getting Your Own Access (It's Free)
===================================

**Slide 3 — about 100 seconds.**

Three steps:

1. **Free ACCESS account** — with your institutional login (CILogon):
   `identity.access-ci.org/new-user <https://identity.access-ci.org/new-user>`_
2. **Allocation** — your PI (or you, as PI) applies through ACCESS. The
   entry-level *Explore ACCESS* tier is designed for small projects, thesis
   work, and classroom use; credits are exchanged for Jetstream2 time.
3. **TACC region** — contact Jetstream2 support
   (`help@jetstream-cloud.org <mailto:help@jetstream-cloud.org>`_ or the
   `contact form <https://jetstream-cloud.org/contact/index.html>`_; an ACCESS
   ticket with *Jetstream2* selected works too) and ask for the **TACC region**
   to be enabled on your allocation.

And the part that matters most:

- **Free for researchers.** Jetstream2 is NSF-funded — no invoices, no credit
  card. Usage draws down your allocation's credits, and shelved or idle VMs
  consume none.

.. note:: Speaker notes (~100 s)

   "You might be wondering how to get this for your own group. It's free —
   genuinely free for researchers; the National Science Foundation pays for
   it. There's no invoicing and no credit card. The process has three steps.
   First, create a free account with ACCESS — the NSF's coordination service —
   using your university's single sign-on; it takes a couple of minutes.
   Second, access to Jetstream2 comes through an ACCESS *allocation*. Your PI
   applies, or adds you to an existing one. The smallest tier, Explore ACCESS,
   is explicitly meant for things like this — small projects, thesis work, and
   classroom use — so a dashboarding group is a natural fit. Third, and
   specific to today: if you want your machines at **TACC** rather than the
   default Indiana region, you just contact Jetstream2 support — email help at
   jetstream-cloud dot org — and ask for the TACC region to be enabled on your
   allocation. That's it. You then log in with your university credentials and
   you're in."
